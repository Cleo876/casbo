Elevating the Ambitious.

The Initiative

C.A.S.B.O is an exclusive, scouted initiative operated jointly by Final Render Animation Studio (F.R.A.S) and Forge. C.A.S.B.O exists to identify small businesses demonstrating exceptional effort, dedication, and a relentless pursuit of professionalism.

Once identified, these businesses are granted a studio-grade technological and creative evolution upgrade—completely free of charge. We look at specific operational facets and execute high-impact upgrades to enhance how they handle and represent themselves to the public.

The Selection

C.A.S.B.O status cannot be applied for. It is earned through visible dedication to your craft and scouted by our team. Selected businesses receive focused, month-long engagements designed to push their brand forward.

The Laurel

Becoming a C.A.S.B.O recipient grants the business the right to display the official Laurel—a permanent, verifiable testament to their standard of excellence and their induction into this elite roster.
