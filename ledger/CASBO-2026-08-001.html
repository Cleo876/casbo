The Mission & Why JANN Was Scouted

As the premier representative body for the animation industry in Jamaica, the Jamaica Animation Nation Network (JANN) carries the responsibility of uniting local animators, studios, educators, and international stakeholders.

When scouting for C.A.S.B.O recipients, we look for organizations demonstrating relentless consistency and community impact that deserve studio-grade digital infrastructure to match their ambition. For an industry-wide network like JANN, official communication isn't just a marketing channel—it is the backbone of institutional legitimacy.

Inside Dispatch-E

Every decision behind Dispatch-E was shaped by two goals: giving JANN real publishing control over how their brand shows up in an inbox, and respecting their need to keep operational overhead at zero.

The Operational Bottleneck

JANN has always focused its resources where they matter most: growing Jamaica’s animation community. Communicating directly through Gmail kept operations lean and avoided recurring software bills, but standard email clients are built for direct correspondence rather than visual publishing. They restrict broadcasts to plain, unformatted text, while commercial newsletter platforms lock structured design behind monthly subscriptions that climb as an audience grows, which is an expense that JANN is trying to manage diligently.

To remove that trade-off, Final Render Animation Studio & Forge engineered Dispatch-E, a custom newsletter application built specifically for JANN.

Engineered around a zero-overhead constraint

Proprietary newsletter platforms tie audience growth to escalating monthly fees, which was not an operational expense JANN could or should have to absorb. Because of that constraint, we built Dispatch-E to operate with $0.00 in recurring platform costs.

To achieve this, we designed an advanced serverless architecture that the application operates on as a framework, removing one of the most expensive parts of running a newsletter system: server maintenance. Utilizing edge computing through Cloudflare Workers and D1, the backend wakes only during executions before going back to sleep in under 50ms. Because JANN also needed scheduled broadcasts to go out even when the desktop app is closed, we paired the edge engine with an automated Google Sheets and Apps Script scheduler that runs on their own account, complete with live health monitoring and Gmail rate-limit protection.

Built-in monetization and partner tracking

A major goal we wanted for JANN is creating a sustainable way to generate revenue by connecting their audience with trusted local brands and opportunities alongside international brands as well. We engineered Dispatch-E with granular link-click tracking, dedicated Promo and Coupon Box blocks, and automatic UTM tagging that solves the "dark traffic" problem in Google Analytics. JANN can now measure exact click-through engagement on partner and affiliate links, giving local sponsors clear, verifiable proof of impact. Even further, the edge engine supports rule-based auto-segmentation—when subscribers click a specific partner link or vote in a One-Click Poll, Dispatch-E can automatically group them into targeted audience segments for focused follow-ups.

Editorial craft built for a pivotal moment

With Jamaica’s new Animation Policy being finalized and moved forward, how JANN communicates carries real public weight. Animators, studios, educators, and government stakeholders need to feel the industry is in capable, organized hands. Dispatch-E gives JANN a full visual workspace with drag-and-drop content blocks, elevated card layouts, personalized merge tags, automatic Table of Contents navigation, hosted "View in Browser" web archives, and custom-branded Subscribe and Unsubscribe pages—all tested for compatibility across Apple Mail, Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, Thunderbird, and ProtonMail. Every dispatch now reads like an official industry publication.

Pioneering JDPA and global privacy compliance

Dispatch-E is the first and only newsletter application built from the ground up to incorporate the Jamaica Data Protection Act (JDPA) directly into its core workflow—alongside GDPR (European Union), UK GDPR (United Kingdom), and CAN-SPAM (United States). Compliance is not an afterthought here; it is enforced by the architecture itself.

Because JANN handles real subscriber data, we engineered local-first credential security using native OS keychain encryption so SMTP passwords and API keys never leave their machine. The system enforces Double Opt-In verification, runs mandatory Pre-Flight compliance checks before any campaign can send, silently scrubs hard bounces, and maintains an immutable, cryptographically hashed audit log (HMAC-SHA256) so consent and deletions can be proven without retaining personal data of unsubscribed users. When a formal record is ever needed, JANN can generate a legally compliant Data Subject Access Request (DSAR) PDF report in a single click, and even the initial Data Processing Agreement is executed and archived as a signed PDF directly inside the app.
